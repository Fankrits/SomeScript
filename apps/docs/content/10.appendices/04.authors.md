# Authors

This guide draws on and adapts material from several freely-licensed sources, alongside original content:

- Andy Roberts' [Getting to Grips with LaTeX](http://www.andy-roberts.net/misc/latex/index.html).
- [Not So Short Introduction to LaTeX2e](http://www.ctan.org/tex-archive/info/lshort/english/lshort.pdf) by Tobias Oetiker, Hubert Partl and Irene Hyna.
- [Getting Started with LaTeX](http://www.maths.tcd.ie/~dwilkins/LaTeXPrimer/) by David Wilkins.
- Peter Flynn's [Formatting Information: A Beginner's Guide to Typesetting with LaTeX](http://www.ctan.org/tex-archive/info/beginlatex/beginlatex-3.6.pdf).
- The [LaTeX Wikibook](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/LaTeX) and its contributors, including Alessio Damato and Pierre Neidhardt, whose work forms the basis of much of this guide's content.

Each of these sources is released under a free license (GFDL and/or GPL, depending on the source) that permits reuse and adaptation.
